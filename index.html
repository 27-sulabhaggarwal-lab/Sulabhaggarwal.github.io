Equity Research Report: Automobile Industry

Aditya Birla Capital · CIP · Apr–Jun 2026

My role: Equity Research Analyst (Corporate Internship Programme). Built and owned the valuation model end to end.

Purpose

Investors needed sector-wide, tax-aware stock calls across India's large-cap automobile OEMs to guide portfolio decisions.

Process

Valued 9 large-cap OEMs (Maruti, Tata Motors, Hyundai, M&M, Bajaj Auto, Hero MotoCorp, Eicher, TVS, Ola Electric/Ather) using EIC analysis, dual P/E and PEG screening, and DCF/FCFF valuation, layered with multi-timeframe technical analysis (RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, DMAs) and post-tax return implications for client portfolios.

Outcome

Delivered a formal report with 4 Buy, 3 Hold, 3 Sell calls, and converted 3 client leads into confirmed investment-linked insurance policies worth ~₹36L in premium value.
